Why herbs fail02 / 09

A herb on a windowsill can fail in four ways.

Most people lose a herb in the first month. Four kinds of failure explain nearly all of it:

Light · Too little

Grows tall and pale

A plant that leans toward the glass and thins out.

Water · Too much

Roots rot

A pot that never dries, with yellow lower leaves.

Heat · Too hot

Leaves scorch

Afternoon sun through a south window in summer.

Care · Too little

Dries out

A pot that is forgotten for a week.

Goal 1 · Keep one herb alive

Name the cheapest setup that keeps a first herb alive for eight weeks.

Goal 2 · Compare the setups

Compare windows and lamps on the same pots, so the result is fair.

Challenges07 / 09

Two challenges: unjudged pots, and a choice only the owner can make.

Key challenge · measuring

Success is hard to measure

Some pots could not be judged. A pot that was moved cannot be scored. A2

So no success rate counts them. They are a challenge, not a result.

Labels are weak. One grower scored most pots.

Business decision · not sure

What do we do with a doubtful pot?

Count it as lost (rule today). Fewer false wins; some good herbs are written off.

Count it as alive. Better totals; the result may flatter the method.

A second grower decides. Fairer, but it needs time.
